ABOUT US

Connected commerce, across Africa.

Network Dynamics began operations in South Africa in 2002. Its principal hub is located in City Deep, Johannesburg, supporting the storage, sales and distribution of fast-moving consumer goods across South Africa and into wider African markets. The business focuses on FMCG storage, sales and distribution. An internal freight operation supports incoming and outgoing containers and full truckload (FTL) movements across northbound, eastbound, westbound and southbound routes. Integrated road, air and sea services connect regional supply chains with local and international consumption markets.
